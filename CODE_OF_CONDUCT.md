# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Free AI Atlas respectful and welcoming regardless of experience, identity, background, ability, nationality, appearance, or personal beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and evidence-focused.
- Critique claims and implementations, not people.
- Respect different skill levels and explain necessary jargon.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Protect private information, credentials, and unpublished reports.
- Accept that maintainers may narrow or decline an entry to preserve quality.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, spam, affiliate manipulation, deceptive promotion, and attempts to distribute leaked keys or unsafe quota-bypass instructions are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, restrict participation, or report conduct to GitHub when needed. For a private conduct report, contact the repository owner through the private contact method shown on their GitHub profile. Do not place sensitive incident details in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and GitHub Community Guidelines.
