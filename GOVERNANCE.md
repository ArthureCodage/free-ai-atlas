# Governance

Free AI Atlas is maintained as a public-interest catalog. Maintainers are stewards of the evidence and contribution process, not owners of facts submitted by the community.

## Decision principles

In order of priority:

1. User safety and legal clarity.
2. Accuracy and reproducible evidence.
3. Genuine usefulness at zero cost.
4. Accessibility for beginners and advanced users.
5. Breadth and publishing speed.

## Roles

- **Contributors** propose resources, corrections, translations, guides, and code.
- **Reviewers** reproduce evidence and check scope, wording, and data consistency.
- **Maintainers** merge changes, manage releases, and enforce project policies.
- **Automation** discovers and validates; it does not own editorial decisions.

## Conflicts and sponsorship

Paid placement is not accepted. A company may contribute a factual entry for its own product, but must disclose the relationship and receives no guarantee of inclusion or favorable wording.

If sponsorship is introduced later, it must be visibly separated from catalog ranking and documented in this file before acceptance.

## Removing resources

A resource can be removed when its free access disappears, evidence becomes unverifiable, the product becomes unsafe or abandoned, the entry duplicates a better route, or its inclusion creates more confusion than value.

## Major changes

Material changes to scope, ranking, licensing, data collection, sponsorship, or automated merge authority should be proposed publicly before implementation.
