## Becoming a category owner

1. Contribute 3+ verified resources or corrections in the category
2. Open an issue requesting ownership
3. Demonstrate understanding of the category's scope and evidence requirements
4. Maintain the category for 3+ months with regular reviews

## Owner responsibilities

- Review contributions to the category within 7 days
- Verify evidence and accuracy of new resources
- Update or remove outdated entries
- Participate in monthly category health reviews
- Help new contributors understand category scope

## Category health

Categories are reviewed monthly for:
- Number of verified resources
- Age of oldest evidence
- Number of open contribution requests
- Owner activity and responsiveness
