# Privacy before prompts

“Free” services can be paid for through limits, product improvement, analytics, or conversion to paid plans. Privacy must be evaluated separately from price.

## Three questions before uploading data

1. **Who can process it?** The product company, model provider, subprocessors, extensions, and connected tools may all be involved.
2. **How is it retained?** Chat history, abuse monitoring, temporary logs, backups, and deleted-account retention can differ.
3. **Can it train or improve a product?** The answer may depend on plan, region, workspace type, or an account setting.

## Default red list

Do not place these in a consumer free service without explicit authorization and suitable terms:

- credentials, API keys, private keys, session cookies;
- health, legal, financial, identity, or biometric data;
- customer records and confidential employer material;
- unreleased source code or security vulnerabilities;
- private conversations without participant consent.

Local inference reduces provider exposure, but extensions, model downloaders, telemetry, cloud fallbacks, and network exposure can reintroduce it.
